# Italus Community Gateway, Rules, and Third-Party Disclosure

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** October 22, 2026  
**Applies to:** Writer's Room, Italus Bookshelf, Manuscript Lab, and Worlds & Characters

## Before You Enter the Community

The Italus community is a place for authors to discuss writing craft, books, works in progress, characters, fictional worlds, and author-directed uses of AI. To protect authors and their work, you must be at least 18 years old, verify control of your email address, and accept these rules before Italus directs you to the community.

The community is hosted by **Beehiiv**, an independent third-party service. Italus Studio operates and moderates the Italus community spaces, while Beehiiv provides the underlying community platform and retains its own moderation and enforcement rights.

By selecting the agreement box and choosing **Agree and continue**, you acknowledge and agree to the following.

## 1. Eligibility and Verified Acceptance

- You confirm that you are at least 18 years old.
- You must verify your email address using the one-time code sent by Italus.
- Italus will record the verified email address, the date and time of acceptance, the version and cryptographic hash of these terms, the selected community destination, and limited security information used to document and protect the acceptance process.
- Your acceptance applies to this version until these terms are materially revised or your community access is revoked. A material revision will require a new verified acceptance.
- A trusted browser may continue to access the gateway without another code for up to 30 days. A new browser, a private browsing session, cleared cookies, an expired trusted session, or a material revision may require another verification code.
- Community verification does not enroll you in marketing email. Newsletter and marketing choices are managed separately.

## 2. Separation of Platforms and Data Practices

- When you continue, you leave ItalusBooks.com and enter a community hosted by Beehiiv.
- Beehiiv processes information needed to provide its community service. Italus processes information needed to operate and moderate the Italus community, document acceptance, protect the gateway, and resolve reports or disputes.
- Your use of Beehiiv is also subject to the [Beehiiv Community Terms](https://www.beehiiv.com/community-terms), [Beehiiv Terms of Use](https://www.beehiiv.com/tou), [Beehiiv Privacy Policy](https://www.beehiiv.com/privacy), and [Beehiiv Acceptable Use Policy](https://www.beehiiv.com/aup).
- Italus cannot control or guarantee Beehiiv's availability, security, retention practices, or independent enforcement decisions. Any limitation of responsibility applies only to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.
- Italus retains community acceptance records for six years after the later of your last community access or the closure of a related dispute, unless a longer period is legally required. Access-event records are ordinarily retained for 12 months. Expired one-time-code records are removed within 24 hours, and expired or revoked trusted-browser sessions are removed within 30 days. Additional information is provided in the [Italus Privacy Policy](https://italusbooks.com/privacy.html).

## 3. Your Content and Privacy

- You retain ownership of original content you post.
- By posting, you give Beehiiv and Italus the limited permissions necessary to host, store, display, reproduce, distribute, review, and moderate that content in connection with operating the community.
- Do not post confidential manuscripts, unreleased proprietary material, private correspondence, passwords, authentication codes, sensitive personal information, or content you do not have permission to share.
- Community content may be visible to other members. Do not treat a community post as a confidential submission to Italus.
- If you share excerpts or creative material for critique, share only what you are comfortable allowing community members and platform operators to view.

## 4. Community Code of Conduct

These rules apply across all Italus community spaces:

- **Writer's Room** — newsletter discussions, studio letters, and monthly creative questions.
- **Italus Bookshelf** — books, research, reviews, recommendations, and reading notes.
- **Manuscript Lab** — excerpts, revision decisions, continuity, and prose craft.
- **Worlds & Characters** — character motives, lore, factions, settings, timelines, and canon.

You may not:

- Harass, bully, threaten, stalk, intimidate, or target another person.
- Post hate speech or attack a person or group based on protected or personal characteristics.
- Publish another person's private or identifying information without permission.
- Impersonate another person or misrepresent your identity or authority.
- Spam, manipulate engagement, distribute malware, attempt unauthorized access, or interfere with the service.
- Post illegal, sexually exploitative, deliberately dangerous, or fraud-facilitating material.
- Plagiarize, misappropriate, or falsely claim ownership of another author's prose, characters, fictional canon, research, artwork, or other protected work.
- Upload content that violates copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, contractual, or other rights.
- Use community material to train, improve, or populate an AI system without the creator's clear permission.
- Evade a suspension or block by using another email address, profile, or account.

Reasoned disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome. Personal attacks and abusive conduct are not.

## 5. Reporting and Intellectual-Property Concerns

- Use Beehiiv's reporting controls when available to report a post or member.
- Reports may also be submitted to Italus using the contact method identified in the community or an enforcement notice.
- A report should identify the content, explain the concern, provide relevant supporting information, and include a way to contact the reporter.
- Italus may preserve relevant records while reviewing a report.
- Italus may remove or restrict disputed content while a review is pending. A moderation decision is not a judicial determination of ownership or legal liability.
- Formal copyright notices must include the information required by applicable law and may also be submitted through Beehiiv's designated process.

## 6. Moderation and Enforcement

- Italus Studio is the administrator and final decision-maker for access to Italus-managed community spaces, subject to Beehiiv's independent rights and applicable law.
- Beehiiv may independently review, restrict, remove, or report content or accounts under its own terms and policies.
- Depending on seriousness, history, and risk, Italus may issue guidance or a warning, remove content, temporarily suspend access, permanently block access, preserve evidence, or refer a matter to Beehiiv or appropriate authorities.
- Severe conduct may result in immediate suspension or permanent removal without a prior warning.
- Possessing an earlier acceptance record or trusted-browser session does not override a current suspension or block.

## 7. Appeals

- If Italus restricts or terminates your access, Italus will send notice to the verified email address when reasonably possible.
- You may appeal within **14 calendar days** after the enforcement notice is sent.
- Follow the appeal instructions in the notice or use the current community contact method published by Italus. Include the affected email address, the decision being appealed, the reason you believe it should be reconsidered, and any relevant supporting information.
- Italus will acknowledge a complete appeal and make a reasonable effort to respond within 14 calendar days. Complex safety, legal, fraud, or intellectual-property matters may require additional time.
- Italus may uphold, modify, or reverse its decision. After the appeal is decided—or if no timely appeal is submitted—the Italus community-access decision is final, subject to applicable law.
- An Italus decision cannot require Beehiiv to restore content or access that Beehiiv independently restricted.

## 8. Changes, Withdrawal, and Contact

- Italus may revise these terms to address changes in the community, law, safety practices, or third-party services.
- Material revisions receive a new version and require a new verified acceptance before the gateway will continue to Beehiiv.
- You may stop using the community at any time. Where applicable, privacy requests concerning Italus records may be submitted through the Italus contact page. Requests concerning Beehiiv-held information must be directed to Beehiiv.
- Questions, reports, and appeals may be submitted through [Italus contact](https://italusbooks.com/contact.html) or the contact instructions in an enforcement notice.

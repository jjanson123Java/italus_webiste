Studio atmosphere

A workspace that can belong to the world you are writing.

Themes change the mood of the studio—not the author controls underneath it. The supplied visual system gives Italus a literary identity across genres.

Original

The literary desk

Deep green, warm brass, parchment, and the quiet focus of a private library.

Mystery

The investigator’s study

Walnut, burgundy, lamplight, and a measured sense of hidden connections.

Fantasy

The worldbuilder’s chamber

Midnight violet, enchanted light, and a place for lore to take root.

Science fiction

The speculative lab

Cyan signals, deep space blues, and a precise environment for new systems.

Designed as focus, not decoration.

Each theme should help authors enter the mood of a project while maintaining readable controls, consistent navigation, and accessible contrast.

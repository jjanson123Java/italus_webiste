Privacy notice

How Italus handles your information

Effective October 22, 2026. This pre-launch notice describes the limited information used by the Italus website, newsletter connection, and verified community gateway.

Community verification

When you request community access, Italus uses Resend to deliver a short-lived email verification code and Neon to store the challenge, trusted-browser session, versioned acceptance evidence, access status, and limited security events. The plaintext code is not stored. Community verification does not enroll you in marketing or change your Writer’s Block newsletter preferences.

Codes expire after 10 minutes and expired code records are removed within 24 hours. A trusted-browser cookie may last 30 days. Expired or revoked session records are removed after 30 days, and ordinary access events after 12 months. Versioned acceptance evidence is retained for at least six years after acceptance and is not automatically deleted at the six-year mark. A related dispute, legal obligation, preservation request, or other legitimate need may require longer retention.

Waitlist and newsletter choices

Waitlist form information is stored in Neon. If you separately check the email-marketing option, Italus sends the subscription request to Beehiiv. Beehiiv controls newsletter confirmation, delivery, pause, and unsubscribe preferences under its own terms and privacy policy.

Third-party services

Italus currently relies on Vercel for hosting and server functions, Neon for database services, Resend for community verification email, and Beehiiv for newsletter and community hosting. Each provider processes information under its own policies.

Your choices

You can cancel the community gateway without accepting, clear the trusted-browser cookie by clearing site data, manage newsletter choices through Beehiiv, or contact Italus about access, correction, or deletion requests where applicable.

Review and contact

This notice and the linked legal terms are pre-launch materials and should receive qualified legal review before commercial launch. Questions and requests may be submitted through the Italus contact page.

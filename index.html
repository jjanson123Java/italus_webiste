The difference

Assistance without authorship drift.

Most AI writing tools begin with a blank prompt. Italus begins with an author’s canon, voice, constraints, and intent—then keeps those decisions in view throughout development.

Author at the center

Draft, edit, reject, resubmit, or accept. The system supports decisions; it does not replace them.

A record of the work

Show how the novel came together.

The Italus creative ledger is designed to record generations, author edits, resubmissions, accepted prose, and token usage—creating a useful history for the author and a clearer provenance story for publishing conversations.

Italus does not claim that its ledger is a legal certification or that every publisher will accept it. It is a transparent project record built for informed conversations.
